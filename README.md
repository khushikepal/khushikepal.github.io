# Khushi's website

A simple personal website, hosted for free with **GitHub Pages**.

## Put it online (free)

1. On GitHub, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to *Deploy from a branch*, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
3. After a minute or two the site is live at:
   **https://khushikepal.github.io**

## Make it yours

Open `index.html` and look for the `<!-- EDIT: ... -->` comments. Replace the
placeholder text with your own intro, interests, projects, and links.
Colors and fonts live in `style.css` (the variables at the top).

You can edit files right on github.com: open a file, click the pencil icon,
make your changes, and commit. The live site updates automatically.
